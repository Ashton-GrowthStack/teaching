# Teaching Project

## Role & Context
ESL Teacher, K3 (ages 3-4) at an IB school in Hangzhou, China.

## Current Focus
- English language teaching focusing on speaking and communication development
- Summer curriculum: Pre-Primer Sight Words (Lavinia Pop structured handwriting program)
- Weekly rotation: a, are, at, away, big (each lesson: letter tracing → word tracing → word writing → sentence completion)

## Key Constraint
**Energy:** Outside life is demanding. Need LOW-ENERGY teaching solutions that still engage K3 students. Avoid high-performance/high-verbal teaching.

## Challenge
Students finding worksheets repetitive/boring. Need to layer engagement (dramatization, movement, games) around structured worksheet sequence while keeping it low-energy.

## Structure
- `/curriculum/` — Sight words programs and materials
- `/activities/` — Classroom activities, games, movement-based learning
- `/resources/` — Worksheets, visual aids, lesson plans

## When Working on This
Suggest activities that maintain curriculum structure but add multisensory/play-based scaffolding. Keep solutions practical and low-energy.
