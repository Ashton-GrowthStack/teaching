# Jade Monkey Curriculum Structure

**Purpose:** Document the progression model, level system, and teaching approach for Jade Monkey reading curriculum with 21 K3 ESL students.

---

## Reading Progression Model

### The Three Steps to Reading Competency

**Step 1: Letter Sounds (Completed)**  
All students have been taught foundational letter sounds.

**Step 2: Letter Blending (90% Mastery)**  
Most students can blend letter sounds into words.  
- B1, B2 groups are still solidifying this skill
- S1-S4 groups have mastered this and moved forward

**Step 3: Jade Monkey Books + Sight Words**  
Students apply blending skills to read Jade Monkey books while learning sight words specific to each book level.
- Each book teaches new sight words
- Students learn sight words IN CONTEXT (within the story)
- Reading fluency increases as sight word knowledge builds
- Students progress through 5 books per level

---

## Level System

### B1 (Pre-Phonics)
- **Skills:** Letter recognition, phoneme awareness
- **Goal:** Build foundation for B2 blending work
- **Focus:** Letter sounds, oral repetition
- **Students:** 2 (Amigo, Lucas)

### B2 (Letter Blending - No Alphablocks)
- **Skills:** Blend letter sounds without visual support (alphablocks)
- **Goal:** Decode CVC words independently
- **Focus:** Blending practice, letter combination patterns
- **Students:** 2 (Ethan, Isla)

### S1 (Jade Monkey Books 1-2 - BOTTLENECK)
- **Skills:** Can blend words but stuck on sight words for these books
- **Goal:** BREAKTHROUGH on books 1-2 sight words
- **Focus:** Intensive sight word instruction, repeated exposure, practice
- **Books:** Jade Monkey Books 1-2 (5 books each)
- **Challenge:** These students have the blending skills but need targeted sight word support
- **Students:** 6 (Jolly, Oscar Deng, Jojo, Cora, Ricky, Max)

### S2 (Jade Monkey Books 3+ - Progressing)
- **Skills:** Solid sight word foundation, moving through mid-level books
- **Goal:** Continue building sight word fluency, advance through levels
- **Focus:** Guided reading, sight word review, reading for comprehension
- **Books:** Jade Monkey Books 3+ (5 books per level)
- **Students:** 5 (Eddie, Yilia, Mila, Wilson, Yanyan)

### S3 (Jade Monkey - Advanced)
- **Skills:** Strong sight word knowledge, fluent decoding
- **Goal:** Maintain fluency, prepare for higher levels
- **Focus:** Independent reading, reading for meaning, assessment
- **Books:** Advanced Jade Monkey levels
- **Students:** 4 (Varun, Oscar M., George, Yimo)

### S4 (Jade Monkey - Highest)
- **Skills:** Fluent sight word recognition, strong reading comprehension
- **Goal:** Independent reading, maintain progress
- **Focus:** Reading fluency checks, book progression, assessment
- **Books:** Highest Jade Monkey levels
- **Students:** 2 (Jasper, Emma)

## Weekly Teaching Structure

### All-Class Worksheet Time (60 min lesson)
Every lesson, all 21 students work on the same **Jade Monkey workbook worksheet** (based on the week/unit).

**Worksheet Sequence (5 days):**
- Day 1: Color by Vowel (phoneme recognition)
- Day 2: Blend and Match (blending practice)
- Day 3: Fill in the Vowel (vowel identification)
- Day 4: Read and Circle Sight Words (sight word recognition + writing)
- Day 5: Sort by Vowels (categorization/review)

**Differentiation:**
- Fast finishers move to Worksheet 2, then Worksheet 3
- Advanced finishers write 10 animals on a blank sheet (open-ended extension)
- All students are productively engaged

### Small Group Rotation (15 min per group, 2 groups per lesson)

While whole class works on worksheets, pull out 2 groups for focused instruction:

| Day | Group | Size | Focus |
|-----|-------|------|-------|
| **Monday** | B1 + B2 | 4 kids | Letter blending foundation, phoneme work |
| **Tuesday** | S3 + S4 | 6 kids | Reading fluency, leveled book practice |
| **Wednesday** | S1 | 6 kids | **SIGHT WORD BREAKTHROUGH** — intensive instruction |
| **Thursday** | S2 | 5 kids | Sight word review, fluency practice |
| **Friday** | Rest | All | Extensions, free reading, games, assessment |

---

## Key Teaching Principles

1. **Low-Energy Delivery**  
   Worksheets provide structure and productivity for all students. Small groups allow targeted help without high-prep burden.

2. **In-Context Sight Word Learning**  
   Sight words are learned through the books themselves, not in isolation. Worksheets reinforce what kids see in stories.

3. **Differentiation Through Pacing**  
   All students do the same worksheet, but at different speeds. Fast finishers keep moving forward.

4. **The S1 Bottleneck**  
   S1 students are NOT behind in ability — they have solid blending skills. They need intensive sight word instruction to unlock books 1-2 and progress. Wednesday is their breakthrough day.

5. **Progression is Individual**  
   Kids move to the next level when they finish a book (5 books per level). No waiting for the group.

---

## Tracking Progress

- **Board Display:** Use whiteboard to show which student is on which book/level (visual, easy to update)
- **Reading Assessments:** 1-on-1 or small group fluency checks during small group time or Friday sessions
- **Sight Word Mastery:** Track via Day 4 worksheet (Read and Circle Sight Words) and Day 5 worksheet
- **Level Advancement:** Student moves to next level when current book is completed with fluency

---

## Troubleshooting

**"A kid is stuck on a book"**  
- Check sight word mastery (Day 4 worksheet)
- Increase sight word practice frequency
- Consider pairing with a higher-level buddy for guided reading
- Move to S1 focused group if not already there

**"Fast finishers are done early"**  
- Worksheet 2 is prepared (extension of Day 1-5)
- Worksheet 3 is for double-finishers
- Animal writing (10 animals) is open-ended and always available
- Consider pairing with a peer for buddy reading

**"I'm overwhelmed with materials"**  
- You already have the worksheets — no additional prep needed
- Small group time uses: flashcards (already exist), the books themselves, or quick sight word games
- TA can help manage materials, display flashcards, run activities during small group time

---

## TA Support

Even with limited English, TA can:
- Display and manage flashcards during sight word instruction
- Run low-language activities (pointing games, movement games)
- Manage classroom behavior while you work with small groups
- Distribute and collect worksheets
- Help fast finishers transition to next worksheet
