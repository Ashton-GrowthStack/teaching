📢 What Do You Like?

Food Preference Phrases - Flashcard Worksheets

K3 ESL - 2 Sessions × 20 Minutes

SESSION 1: Question Phrases (Repeat & Memorize)

Goal: Students learn to ask "What do you like for...?" using choral repetition and gesture anchors.

What do you like for breakfast?
What do you like for snack?
What do you like for lunch?
What do you like for tea?
What do you like for dinner?
What do you like at bedtime?
🎯 ACTIVITY: Response Phrases
Match the question to the food response:
I like cereal
I like toast
I like yoghurt
I like an apple
I like an egg
I like cake

🎮 GAME IDEAS FOR PRACTICE:
1. Speed Drill: Show flashcard for 3 seconds, students call out the word
2. Call & Response: Teacher says "I like..." students complete with food word
3. Gesture Game: Each food has a gesture - choral drills with actions
4. Question Chain: Point to student → "What do you like?" → Student responds
