🎭 GESTURE & RHYTHM GUIDE

Breakfast Foods - SLOW & SMOOTH

  • 🥣 cereal - Circle hand motion (pouring)
  • 🍞 toast - Pop hand up (like toast popping)
  • 🥛 yoghurt - Spoon motion (eating)
  • 🥚 egg - Crack hand gesture

Lunch & Dinner - MEDIUM SPEED

  • 🍎 apple - Point & pick motion
  • 🥪 sandwich - Clap hands together
  • 🍕 pizza - Triangle hand shape
  • 🥔 potato - Hold up fist (round)
  • 🍝 pasta - Twirl fork motion

Bedtime Foods - SOFT & GENTLE

  • 🍌 banana - Peel motion with hands
  • 🍪 biscuit - Bite motion
  • 🥛 milk - Cup hands (holding glass)
  • 🍫 chocolate - Rubbing hands together (warm)
