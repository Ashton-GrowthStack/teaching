FOUNDATION LEVEL

18-Week Oxford Phonics Reading Curriculum

Phonics Instruction Program | 10-15 Minutes Daily

Program Summary: This 18-week curriculum provides structured phonics instruction for Foundation level learners. Students progress from short vowel sounds through complex long vowel patterns, with systematic review weeks to ensure mastery.

Duration: 18 weeks (6 books × 3 weeks per book)

Instructional Time: 10-15 minutes per day

Structure: 8 weeks of focused instruction + 1 review week, repeated twice

UNIT 1: SHORT VOWELS

Weeks 1-8 introduce the five short vowel sounds through consonant-vowel-consonant (CVC) word patterns. Week 9 provides comprehensive review and assessment.

Week Book Focus Content & Example Words
1-2 Book 1 Short A cat, bat, rat, mat, hat, pan, can, man, ran, bag, tag, rag, dam, jam, ram, yam, cap, tap, map, nap
3 Book 2 Short E pen, hen, ten, bed, red, fed, net, wet, set, leg, peg, beg
4 Book 2 Short I big, dig, fig, pig, wig, pin, fin, bin, win, bit, fit, hit, kit, sit, zip, lip, tip
5 Book 3 Short O dog, fog, hog, log, hop, mop, pop, top, cot, dot, got, hot, pot
6 Book 3 Short U bug, hug, jug, mug, rug, bun, fun, run, sun, cup, but, cut, hut, nut
7-8 Book 4 Consonant Blends flag, stop, step, slip, grab, plant, blast, strap, split, blend with all 5 short vowels
9 — REVIEW WEEK Assessment, consolidation, and fluency practice with all short vowel patterns

Short Vowel Learning Outcomes

By the end of Week 9, students will:

  • Identify and produce all 5 short vowel sounds
  • Blend CVC words fluently
  • Recognize consonant blend patterns
  • Read simple sentences containing short vowel words

UNIT 2: LONG VOWELS

Weeks 10-17 introduce long vowel patterns through three main categories: silent E, vowel digraphs, and special letter combinations. Week 18 provides comprehensive review and celebration of reading growth.

Week Book Pattern Content & Example Words
10 Book 4 Long A (a_e) cape, cane, cake, game, gate, lake, make, name, take, wave, bake, came, date, fate
11 Book 5 Long I (i_e) bike, kite, like, line, time, hide, hike, fire, five, nine, pine, pile, rice, ride
12 Book 5 Long O & U (o_e, u_e) bone, home, hope, rope, cone, dove, cube, cute, mule, tube, dune, June, tune, rule
13 Book 5 AI & AY Digraphs rain, mail, pain, train, brain, aid, brain, day, play, say, way, bay, gray, hay, stay
14 Book 6 EE & EA Digraphs bee, tree, see, feed, feel, meet, keep, beach, team, dream, sea, eat, heat, meat
15 Book 6 IGH, IE, Y Patterns light, night, high, sight, fight, pie, tie, lie, sky, cry, my, happy, silly, baby, candy
16 Book 6 OA & OW Digraphs boat, coat, road, toad, goat, snow, grow, show, low, flow, know, window, yellow
17 Book 6 UE, UI, EW, OO Patterns blue, glue, clue, fruit, suit, juice, new, few, grew, drew, moon, food, pool, boot
18 — REVIEW WEEK Assessment, consolidation, and celebration of all long vowel patterns and reading growth

Long Vowel Learning Outcomes

By the end of Week 18, students will:

  • Identify and produce long vowel sounds using multiple patterns
  • Recognize and apply the silent E rule
  • Blend words with vowel digraphs and special letter combinations
  • Read and comprehend sentences and simple texts with long vowel words
  • Apply phonics knowledge to decode unfamiliar words independently

DAILY PHONICS PRACTICE (10-15 Minutes)

Each lesson incorporates the following components:

Component Description
Phoneme Introduction Explicit instruction of the target sound through isolation, oral practice, and comparison with known sounds (Days 1-2)
Word Building Guided practice creating words using letter combinations; blending practice with visual support (Days 3-4)
Application & Fluency Reading words in context, sentence building, writing practice, speed drills (Days 5+)

PROGRESS MONITORING

Formative Assessment (Ongoing):

  • Observation of student blending accuracy and fluency
  • Daily completion and accuracy of word-building activities
  • Student engagement in reading tasks

Summative Assessment (Review Weeks 9 & 18):

  • Short vowel fluency check (Week 9)
  • Long vowel pattern recognition (Week 18)
  • Reading comprehension with phonetically regular texts
  • Ability to decode unfamiliar words independently

SUPPORTING LEARNING AT HOME

Parents can reinforce phonics instruction through:

  • Daily shared reading with a focus on sounding out words
  • Identifying phonics patterns in environmental print (signs, labels, books)
  • Playing rhyming and word-building games
  • Praising effort and persistence in reading

This curriculum is designed to provide systematic, explicit phonics instruction aligned with the Oxford Phonics World program.
