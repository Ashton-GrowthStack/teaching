PROFICIENT LEVEL

18-Week Oxford Phonics Reading Curriculum

Phonics Instruction Program | 10-15 Minutes Daily

Program Summary: This 18-week curriculum provides advanced phonics instruction for Proficient level learners. Students progress through long vowel patterns and complex consonant blend combinations, preparing them for fluent reading.

Duration: 18 weeks (6 books × 3 weeks per book)

Instructional Time: 10-15 minutes per day

Structure: 8 weeks of focused instruction + 1 review week, repeated twice

UNIT 1: LONG VOWELS (ADVANCED)

Weeks 1-8 reinforce and extend long vowel patterns from Foundation level, with emphasis on multiple representations. Week 9 provides comprehensive review and assessment.

Week Book Focus Content & Example Words

9 — REVIEW WEEK Assessment and consolidation of all long vowel patterns; fluency practice with complex vowel combinations

Long Vowel Learning Outcomes

  • Recognize and apply multiple representations of long vowel sounds
  • Distinguish between silent E pattern and vowel digraph patterns
  • Read multi-syllable words containing long vowels
  • Develop fluency with complex vowel combinations

UNIT 2: CONSONANT BLENDS & DIGRAPHS

Weeks 10-17 introduce complex consonant blends and digraphs, building on Foundation-level blending skills. Week 18 provides comprehensive review and reading fluency assessment.

18 — REVIEW WEEK Assessment and consolidation of all consonant blends and digraphs; fluency practice with complex syllable structures

Consonant Blend & Digraph Learning Outcomes

  • Recognize and blend all initial consonant blends fluently
  • Identify and decode digraph combinations (SH, CH, TH, etc.)
  • Read and decode words with final blend clusters
  • Apply knowledge of soft consonant sounds in context
  • Decode multi-syllable words with complex consonant patterns

DAILY PHONICS PRACTICE (10-15 Minutes)

Component Description
Pattern Review & Contrast Explicit instruction of the target pattern with comparison to previously learned patterns (Days 1-2)
Word Decoding Guided practice decoding words using the target blend or digraph; isolation and blending practice (Days 3-4)
Fluency & Application Reading words in context, sentence building, comprehension practice, speed drills (Days 5+)

PROGRESS MONITORING

Formative Assessment: Daily observation of decoding accuracy, blending fluency, pattern recognition

Summative Assessment (Weeks 9 & 18): Long vowel pattern fluency, consonant blend accuracy, reading comprehension with complex text

SUPPORTING LEARNING AT HOME

  • Read together daily with focus on multi-syllable word decoding
  • Identify blends and digraphs in environmental print and texts
  • Practice oral blending of complex sound combinations
  • Discuss meaning of words with multiple consonant clusters

This curriculum is designed to provide systematic instruction in advanced phonics patterns aligned with the Oxford Phonics World program.
